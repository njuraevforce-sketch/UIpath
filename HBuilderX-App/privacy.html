Privacy, in your own space.

Original Diary Privacy Policy
Effective date: October 29, 2026

Who operates the app

Publisher: njuraevforce-sketch. Contact: n.juraevforce@gmail.com.

Your entries stay on your device

Diary entries, titles, dates, moods, favorite selections, selected illustrations, your optional profile name and preferences are stored locally. In the standalone iOS app, a mirrored copy is also written to the application support container using atomic writes and system file protection. The diary application does not upload this information to a developer-operated server. An account is not required.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The diary web application and the included standalone Swift/WKWebView iOS source contain no advertising SDK, analytics service or cross-app tracking code. No tracking permission is requested. If you choose a different wrapper or add SDKs, its data practices must be reviewed before release.

PWA hosting and offline use

When you open the hosted PWA, your browser requests the application files and bundled illustrations from its hosting provider. Standard hosting access logs may contain your IP address, user agent, request time and requested file path. Diary text is not included in these requests. After its offline cache is ready, the PWA can run without an internet connection. The bundled iOS project includes these resources locally.

Backups, import and sharing

Export is initiated by you. A backup can contain your entries, optional profile name and illustration selections. Files or text you choose to share are handled by the destination application or service under its own privacy policy. Imported files are processed locally. Temporary export files in the standalone iOS wrapper are deleted after the share sheet completes or is dismissed.

Support

If you email support, your email address and anything you choose to include will be received by the mailbox provider. Do not send private diary content unless you want to share it. Support correspondence is used to respond to your request; request its deletion by contacting the same address, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Retention and deletion

Your diary data remains in local application or browser storage until you delete it, clear site data or uninstall the app. You can delete individual entries or use Profile → Settings → Delete all data. The latter clears the diary entries and profile from the current installation, but does not delete backup copies you saved elsewhere. Browser and native installations may have separate storage.

Security and device backups

The diary does not provide its own encryption layer for local web storage. Protect your device with a passcode and keep your operating system updated. Device and cloud backups may be handled by Apple or your device provider according to your settings. We cannot restore entries that were not exported or backed up.

Children and permissions

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or advertising permissions. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated account or server.

Changes and questions

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in an updated policy and effective date. Contact n.juraevforce@gmail.com with privacy questions.
