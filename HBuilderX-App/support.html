A little help for your diary.

Questions, a bug or a privacy request? Email n.juraevforce@gmail.com.

How do I save an entry?

Tap Write, add a title or some text, then tap Done. Your entry is saved on this device.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The PWA needs one successful online load to cache its files. The included iOS bundle includes its files from the first launch.

How do I back up my writing?

Open Profile → Export entries and save the JSON file or backup text outside the app. To restore it, choose Import entries.

Can I move to another device?

Export a backup on the old device and import it on the new one. There is no automatic cloud sync.

How do I remove my data?

Use Profile → Settings → Delete all data. Delete separately any backup files saved to other apps or cloud drives.

Please include your device model, operating system and app version when reporting a problem. Do not include private diary text or passwords.
