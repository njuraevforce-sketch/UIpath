# Screenshots for App Store Connect

Capture screenshots from the signed iOS/TestFlight app on supported iPhone/iPad sizes accepted by App Store Connect at submission time. Do not upload browser development screenshots as if they were verified native screenshots.

Suggested English screens:

1. Home — "Every day, your story." with the original illustrated cover carousel.
2. Entry editor — a fictional example such as "A little golden hour"; show mood and cover controls.
3. Entries — fictional entries with covers and favorites; no real private diary content.
4. Calendar — the same fictional entry shown on its date.
5. Profile or backup flow — show the real settings, not financial/NFT functionality from the reference app.

Screenshots should match the release build, its name and icon. Avoid promises of financial returns, marketing for unrelated apps or claims of encryption not implemented by this app.

Native screenshots have not been generated in this Linux environment. On-device capture and dimension verification remain release steps for the publisher.
